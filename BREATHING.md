# Breathing

Using one hand, count to 5 one finger at a time. Don't go too fast, don't go too slow.

Breathe In - Count to 5 on your hand
Hummmmmmm - Count down from 5 on your hand
Breathe In - Count to 5 on your hand
Breathe Out - Count down from 5 on your hand

1 Session = Repeat 7 times

Repeat 3 - 6 Sessions Per Day
