# Breathing

Using one hand, count to 5 one finger at a time. Don't go too fast, don't go too slow.

- Breathe In &mdash; Count to 5 on your hand
- Hummmmmmm &mdash; Count down from 5 on your hand
- Breathe In &mdash; Count to 5 on your hand
- Breathe Out &mdash; Count down from 5 on your hand

1 Session = Repeat 7 times

Repeat 3 &mdash; 6 Sessions Per Day
